# SPARK Workflow Feature List

## Procedure Setup
- **Document Upload:** Uploading procedure documents via the user interface (ZIP / PDF).
- **Content Extraction:** Extraction of contents from uploaded documents as a basis for subsequent review steps.
- **Integration of External Interfaces:** Connection of external interfaces such as the Specialist Planning Portal (FPP) for data transfer.

## Formal Completeness Check
- **AI-Supported Document Assignment:** Automatic assignment of each document to document type and topics.
- **Comparison Against Table of Contents:** Review of uploaded documents against the applicant's table of contents.
- **Comparison Against Official Requirements:** Review against officially required documents.
- **Request for Missing Documents:** Missing documents are identified as a notice and can be requested directly.

## Plausibility Check
- **Contradiction Detection Within Documents:** AI-supported detection of contradictions within individual documents.
- **Contradiction Detection Between Documents:** AI-supported detection of contradictions between each document and the explanatory report.
- **Reference Documentation and Requests:** Each finding is documented with references. Requests can be easily created.

## Material Completeness Check
- **Material Completeness Check:** Topic-based review for trigger effect through norm-based AI search for supporting references in planning documents with references to the respective legal norm.
- **Legal Changes:** Consideration of legal changes through connection to legal databases and notification of changes in ongoing procedures.

## Authority and Public Participation
- **Thematic Categorization of Participation Submissions:** Automatic decomposition of participation submissions into individual arguments and categorization by topics and subtopics.
- **Export and Import Function:** Export of segmented participation submissions as CSV file and import of responses from the project sponsor to the participation submissions.

## Legal Review and Assessment
- **Topic- and Norm-Based AI Assessment:** AI assessment of the relevant legal basis (e.g., fulfilled / not fulfilled) with detailed review results with references as justification support.
- **Digital Legal Dogmatics:** Deconstruction of norms into their legal methodological components such as elements of facts or indeterminate legal concepts as well as identification of references.

## Decision Drafting
- **Decision Drafting in Integrated Editor:** Drafting the decision in the integrated editor along the authority-specific structure.
- **Decision Export in PDF/DOCX:** Export of the decision as Word or PDF file in the stored format template (official letterhead).
- **AI Risk Indicator:** AI risk indicator for quality assurance of decision chapters (severity levels low / medium / high).

## Secondary Functions
- **Request for Application Documents:** Requests are structured and can be exported in consolidated form.
- **Commenting:** Comments can be set per review step.
- **Deadline and Schedule Management:** Deadlines can be stored and monitored per procedure.
- **Logging:** Logging of all relevant processes in the system.
- **Plan Changes:** Function for versioning modified documents.
- **Job Status Display:** Display of the current status of running processing jobs.
- **Role and Rights Concept:** Processing roles can be assigned authority- and procedure-specifically.
- **Redaction Agent:** Function for automated redaction of sensitive personal data.
- **Approvals for Procedures:** Control of approvals within the procedure by authorized roles.
- **Feedback Mechanisms for AI Analyses:** Feedback on AI results for quality assessment.
- **Multilingualism:** Support of German and English languages in the user interface.
