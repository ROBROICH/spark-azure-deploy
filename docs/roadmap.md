# SPARK Open-Source Roadmap
This document describes the phased open-source publication of SPARK Workflow.
## Release overview
|Release|Functional focus|Operational scope|
|-|-|-|
|First Release|Initial preprocessing and early procedural support|Covers document intake, extraction, and initial formal and plausibility checks|
|Second Release|Substantive and legal review and assessment, authority and public participation, cross-cutting operational functions, and deployment assets|Completes the open-source publication|
### First Release — 2026-03-31
Release one provides the core preprocessing and early-stage procedural support capabilities of SPARK.
Included scope:
* Content extraction from application documents
* Formal completeness checks
* Plausibility checks
* Supporting backend services
### Second Release — 2026-06-15
> Note: Releases two and three were originally planned as separate phases. They are now published together in a single release.

Release two adds substantive completeness check, legal review and assessment, and the drafting of decisions, together with the participation of public authorities and the public, cross-cutting operational functions, and deployment assets. From this release onward, SPARK supports the complete handling of applications. This release completes the open-source publication. The main frontend is published with this release.
Included scope:
* Material completeness check
* Legal review and assessment
* Drafting of decisions
* Risk reporter (part of drafting of decisions)
* Handling of legal changes
* Handling of plan changes
* Job status display
* Authority and public participation
* Roles and rights management
* Approval workflows
* User management
* Logging
* Personal information redaction function
* Feedback mechanism
* Frontend
* Complete backend services
* Legal dogmatics
* Helm charts for deployment
